Illucet 1.1.0 expands bulk downloading, website support and account controls on iPhone and iPad.

## What's new

- **Bulk URL imports.** Paste text from Notes or import TXT, Markdown, CSV, HTML and RTF documents. Review recognized links, remove duplicates, select media and queue mixed images and videos from different sites. Imports support up to 500 links, with item-specific download options and failed-link export.
- **More websites.** Added download routes for TikTok, Instagram, X, Reddit, Facebook, Bluesky, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Twitch, SoundCloud and Pinterest alongside YouTube. Available media and formats depend on the post and its website.
- **Post images and carousels.** Download supported photo posts and mixed photo/video posts, preserving original image formats where available. Optional image metadata removal is available for supported formats.
- **Accounts by website.** The Accounts page groups browser sign-in and cookie imports by service, shows connected accounts and supports multiple profiles. Existing YouTube account profiles migrate to the new layout.
- **Guest-first account use.** Automatic starts with guest extraction and uses the preferred account only when the service explicitly requires access, such as private or age-restricted media. Guest-only and explicit account options are available.
- **Download engine choices.** Choose Native, yt-dlp or the optional aria2 engine, including per-site defaults. Automatic retains the native direct-transfer path. aria2 supports public HTTP/HTTPS files through the existing queue and output verification.
- **YouTube compatibility controls.** Select the JavaScript solver and client, use Apple WebKit when appropriate, and generate supported PO tokens locally on demand while the app is open.
- **Share Sheet queueing.** The bundled Download with Illucet Shortcut can submit multiple links to the download queue, retain the selected preferences and save work for continuation when iOS limits execution time.

## Reliability and compatibility

- Improved mixed-batch restoration and per-item configuration, source identity checks, cancellation cleanup and network-policy handling.
- Verified a live five-site batch of Pinterest, Bluesky and X images plus YouTube and Facebook videos with aria2 selected. All five saved outputs decoded successfully. System Share Sheet queue admission and restoration were checked separately with fixtures.
- Updated dependency notices and native source packaging for the bundled image adapters, local token provider and aria2.

## Known limits

- Websites can require legitimate account access, reject connections or change their extraction requirements. A recognized link may still be unavailable. A Vimeo sample encountered a service challenge before transfer. Reddit discovery and refresh require foreground access to its post page.
- aria2 and yt-dlp require in-process execution time. Incomplete transfers restart after interruption. Native remains the default for direct transfers. Background completion depends on iOS scheduling.
- Broad private-account access and physical-device background execution have limited verification. DRM-protected content, ongoing live recording and whole-profile or board archives are unsupported.

## Installation

Requires iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. The release IPA is unsigned and requires your own sideloading/signing setup. The release includes third-party notices, corresponding native source inputs and SHA-256 checksums. Illucet's application license is GPL-3.0.
